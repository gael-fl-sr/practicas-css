Daily Racoon

September 30th, 1998

THE DEAD WALK!

Martial Law Declared as Unidentified Infections Turn Citizens Violent

By Gael Flores

RACCOON CITY — Riots and mass hysteria have gripped the streets as an unidentified, highly contagious disease transforms ordinary citizens into violent attackers. The Raccoon Police Department (R.P.D.) has officially declared martial law effective immediately, urging all residents to barricade themselves indoors. Local authorities are completely overwhelmed, with emergency services failing to respond as the body count continues to rise exponentially across the metropolitan area.

Eyewitnesses describe horrifying scenes of blood-stained streets and individuals exhibiting cannibalistic behavior. Raccoon General Hospital has ceased admissions after multiple patients and staff members mysteriously turned on each other. As telephone lines go dead and power grids begin to fail, scattered R.P.D. officers and the specialized S.T.A.R.S. unit are reportedly making a desperate final stand at the central police station, though radio contact remains highly unstable.
